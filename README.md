# Exploratory Data Analysis of Twitch Streamers (R)

**Which strategies help a new Spanish-speaking streamer grow? A data-driven exploration of the top 1,000 Twitch channels.**

![](images/Twitch_Main.png)

## Context

Twitch is the leader in live streaming, especially for video games. A close friend of mine started streaming as a hobby and dreams of making a living from it. This project analyses platform data to find the patterns, behaviours and strategies that help a streamer stand out, and turns them into practical, evidence-based recommendations.

**Key question:** how can a streamer, especially in the early years, identify and prioritise the strategies that maximise growth on Twitch?

## Data

- **Source:** Kaggle, [Top 1000 Twitch Streamers Data (May 2024)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/hibrahimag1/top-1000-twitch-streamers-data-may-2024).
- **File:** [`dataset/datasetV2.csv`](dataset/datasetV2.csv), **999 streamers x 17 variables** (language, most streamed game, average stream duration, followers gained per stream, average viewers, total views, most active day...).
- **Scope:** the whole dataset is explored first; the bivariate analysis then focuses on the **Spanish-speaking community (106 streamers)**, the language of my friend's audience.

## Main findings

- **English dominates** the top 1,000 (40.1%) but **Spanish is 10.6%**.
- **Just Chatting** is the most common main category (25.7%), followed by League of Legends, GTA V and Valorant.
- **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday** concentrate 50% of streamers' most active days; among Spanish-speaking streamers the most active days are Tuesday, Wednesday and Sunday, and **Wednesday** gets the most views.
- Among Spanish-language streamers the **median stream is about 4 hours** (mean 4.4); duration shows no strong correlation with viewers or followers.
- Numeric variables are at most **moderately correlated** (strongest: total views vs total followers, r = 0.65), so no single variable explains growth.
- **Recommendations:** stream 4-5 hours, favour Just Chatting / League of Legends / GTA V (with Minecraft as a hypothesis to test), focus on Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday, and promote the channel with clips on other platforms.

**Limitations:** a single snapshot of already-successful channels (survivorship bias), many game categories with only one or a few streamers, and moderate correlations that do not imply causation. The notebook says so explicitly.
